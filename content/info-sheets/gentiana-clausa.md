### aka closed gentian, meadow bottle gentian

## _Gentiana clausa_

**Native to:** All of New England

**Life cycle:** Perennial

**Light**: Full Sun to Full Shade

**Soil Moisture:** Wet to Medium-Wet

**Height**: 10-18"<br />**Spacing**: 1-2’

**Blooms**: Late summer<br />
**Bloom Color:** Blue, Purple

**Natural Habitat:** Wet woods and meadows

**Benefits**: Attracts Bees, Attracts Butterflies, Meadow garden, Rain Garden

**Pairs Well With:** Blue lobelia, golden groundsel, cranesbill geranium, blue iris, swamp milkweed, and tall white aster 

> "Upon first encountering this plant, one wonders when the flowers will finally open. Eventually you realize the flower 'buds' you are inspecting are the mature flowers--the proof comes in watching pollinators such as bumblebees forcing their way into the 'bottle.'"
> 
> -- Native Plant Trust
