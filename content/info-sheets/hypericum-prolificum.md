## *Hypericum prolificum*

**Native to:** New York and further west/south

**Life cycle:** Perennial

**Light**: Full/Partial Sun

**Soil Moisture:** Medium-Wet to Medium-Dry

**Height**: 2-5’<br />
**Spacing**: 3-4’

**Blooms**: Jul-Sep<br />
**Bloom Color:** Yellow

**Natural Habitat:** Open woods, meadows, and roadsides

**Benefits**: Pollinator Favorite, Bird Favorite, Deer/Rabbit Resistant, Host Plant (eg. grey hairstreak) Drought Tolerant, Urban Environment, Salt Tolerant, Erosion Control/Soil Stabilization, Low Maintenance

**Pairs Well With:** Culver's root, heart-leaved Alexander, nodding onion, three-lobed coneflower, and flowering raspberry
