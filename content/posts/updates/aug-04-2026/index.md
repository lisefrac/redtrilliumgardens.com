Sadly, I am late to bring you this month's market schedule. But there are still plenty of markets ahead of us this month!

(And be sure scroll to the end of this post for a special announcement...)

## Beverly Garden Party

In conjunction with Grow Native Mass, we will be at [Beverly's Garden Party](https://beverly400.org/garden-party/) this upcoming **Saturday, August 8, 2026, 12-5pm**, on the Beverly Common at 31 Dane Street, Beverly, MA! This event celebrates the 400th anniversary of the city of Beverly -- that's pretty old!

We will be joined by Checkerspot Farm of Colrain, MA with their lovely selection of natives. Between the two of us, you're sure to find that special plant!

{{< figure src="images/beverly-garden-party-2026.jpg" alt="Native plant popup market at Beverly Garden Party - Saturday, August 8, 2026, 12pm-5pm, Beverly Common, 31 Dane St, Beverly, MA">}}

## Devens Farmers' Market

Recently added to our market schedule is [Devens Farmers' Market:](https://www.devensec.com/news/Farmers_Market_2026_Dates_Redux.pdf) on **Tuesday, August 25, 11:30am - 2:3pm**, on the corner of Elm and Jackson Streets, Devens, MA. I'm excited about this one because it's so close to me! This is another Grow Native Mass popup market, and this time we'll be joined by Hilltop Natives! 

## Save the date for something special...

Big news is coming for Red Trillium Gardens -- and we'll be kicking it off with a market in Berlin, MA. The details are still being finalized, but save the date for Saturday, August 22, 2026, from 11am to 5pm. 

Hope to see you in August!
